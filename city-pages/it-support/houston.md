# Top IT Support Companies in Houston, TX

## What Houston IT Services Cover
Houston’s IT support ecosystem is as diverse as the city itself. Most providers offer a blend of on‑premise and cloud‑based solutions tailored to protect, streamline, and scale businesses of all sizes. Typical services include:

- **Network design and management** – setting up secure LAN/WANs, Wi‑Fi coverage across multi‑floor office spaces, and VPNs for remote workers.
- **Cybersecurity** – firewalls, endpoint protection, phishing simulations, and regular penetration testing to stay ahead of evolving threats.
- **Cloud services** – migration to Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or private cloud setups on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud, plus integration with existing on‑prem systems.
- **Data backup & disaster recovery** – automated nightly backups, off‑site replication, and tested recovery plans that satisfy HIPAA, PCI, or other industry compliance needs.
- **Hardware & software lifecycle management** – procurement, installation, patching, and end‑of‑life planning for desktops, servers, printers, and mobile devices.
- **Help‑desk and ticketing** – 24/7 remote support, on‑site visits, and a knowledge base that reduces downtime for employees.
- **Strategic IT consulting** – roadmap development, vendor negotiations, and cost‑optimization studies to align technology with business goals.

Because Houston hosts a mix of manufacturing, medical, energy, and tech firms, IT providers often customize these services to meet sector‑specific regulations and performance requirements.

## How to Choose a Provider
- **Licensing & insurance** – verify state business registration, professional liability, and workers’ compensation.
- **Industry certifications** – look for CompTIA A+/Network+, Microsoft Certified: Azure/AWS, Cisco CCNA/CCNP, or ISO 27001 compliance.
- **Customer reviews & case studies** – check Google, Yelp, and industry forums for recent testimonials that match your sector.
- **Written estimates** – compare detailed proposals that break down labor, equipment, and recurring costs.
- **Response time & SLA** – confirm on‑site availability (e.g., <4 hrs) and remote response windows (e.g., <30 min).
- **Warranties & support terms** – ensure hardware warranties, software license renewal policies, and clear escalation paths.

## Typical Costs and What Affects Price
- **Hourly rates** for on‑site support generally fall between **$50–$150** per hour, depending on specialty and urgency.
- **Managed services contracts** are priced **$1,000–$3,000 per month** for small‑to‑mid‑size businesses, with larger enterprises scaling higher based on user count and infrastructure complexity.
- **Project‑based work** (e.g., cloud migration or network redesign) usually ranges from **$5,000 to $25,000**, influenced by scope, data volume, and required downtime windows.
- **Factors that drive price** include:
  - **Business size** (number of users, devices, and locations)
  - **Industry compliance** (HIPAA, PCI, NIST)
  - **Level of monitoring** (24/7 vs. business hours)
  - **On‑site vs. remote support mix**
  - **Contract length** (long‑term discounts are common)

These bands provide a realistic baseline; actual quotes will vary based on your unique requirements.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring
- What certifications do your technicians hold, and how often are they refreshed?
- What is your average first‑response time for critical incidents, and what is the escalation process?
- How do you handle data backup, retention, and disaster‑recovery drills?
- Can you provide references from Houston businesses in the same industry?
- What are the terms for hardware warranty, software licensing, and ongoing support?
