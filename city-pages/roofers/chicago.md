# Find Trusted Roofing Contractors in Chicago, IL

## What Chicago Roofers Services Cover  
Chicago’s climate—frost‑free winters, humid summers, and occasional hailstorms—means a roof must resist a range of stresses.  Most roofing contractors in the Wind City offer a full spectrum of services, including:

- **Residential and commercial roof installation** for asphalt shingles, metal panels, slate, and flat‑roof systems.  
- **Inspection and maintenance** to spot leaks, protect underlayment, and extend roof life.  
- **Repair work** for damaged shingles, flashing, vents, and drainage components.  
- **Storm‑damage restoration**—quick shut‑down of leaks, replacement of tiles, and structural assessments after hail or wind events.  
- **Energy‑efficient upgrades** such as cool‑roof coatings, solar‑panel mounting, and ventilation improvements.  
- **Specialty services**: roof cleaning, mold remediation, and fire‑proofing for older homes or industrial sites.

Because Chicago’s building codes evolve, many local roofers stay current on the Illinois Roofing & Building Code, ensuring that every project meets safety, fire‑resistance, and energy‑efficiency standards.

## How to Choose a Provider  
When comparing roofers, use this checklist to screen candidates quickly:

- **Licensing & Insurance** – Verify the contractor holds a valid Illinois roofing license and carries comprehensive liability and workers’‑comp insurance.  
- **Local Reputation** – Look for at least 3‑year history in the Chicago area, read 5‑star reviews on Google, Yelp, and Angi, and ask for recent customer references.  
- **Written Estimates** – Get a detailed, itemized quote that lists materials, labor, permits, and cleanup. A clear estimate protects against hidden fees.  
- **Response Time** – A prompt reply (within 24 hours) and a scheduled on‑site visit show professionalism and respect for your schedule.  
- **Warranty Coverage** – Confirm the contractor offers a combined manufacturer and workmanship warranty, and know the exact duration and coverage limits.  

Evaluate each factor against the others; a single weak link can compromise the whole job.

## Typical Costs and What Affects Price  
Roofing costs in Chicago vary by material, roof size, and complexity of the job.  In 2026, you can expect the following broad ranges:

| Roof Type | Approx. Cost Range (per square, 100 sq ft) |
|-----------|-------------------------------------------|
| Asphalt shingle | $300 – $550 |
| Metal panel | $500 – $900 |
| Slate | $800 – $1,600 |
| Flat‑roof (EPDM, TPO) | $400 – $700 |

Key price influencers:

- **Roof area** – Larger roofs require more materials, labor, and time.  
- **Pitch and structure** – Steep or oddly shaped roofs need scaffolding or safety equipment, adding cost.  
- **Material quality** – Premium shingles or metal panels carry higher upfront prices but offer longer warranties.  
- **Site access** – Roofs on high‑rise buildings or in congested neighborhoods may incur additional labor or equipment fees.  
- **Permitting & inspections** – Local ordinances in Chicago can add a few hundred dollars for permit processing and final inspections.  

Always ask the contractor to explain how each factor is reflected in the estimate.

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring  
Before signing, clarify these points with the roofer:

- **What specific materials will be used, and do they match the manufacturer’s warranty?**  
- **Can you provide a written, itemized invoice and proof of licensing/licensing?**  
- **How long will the project take, and what are the daily progress checkpoints?**  
- **What cleanup and debris removal policy do you follow?**  
- **Do you handle permits and inspections, and can you show proof of compliance with Chicago Building Codes?**

These questions help you gauge professionalism, protect your investment, and ensure a smooth project timeline.
