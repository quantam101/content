# Find Trusted Roofing Contractors in Los Angeles, CA

## What Los Angeles Roofers Services Cover  
Los Angeles roofers tackle a wide range of projects tailored to the city’s diverse climate and building styles. The most common services include:  

- **Full‑roof installation** for new construction and major additions, often using asphalt shingles, metal panels, or architectural slate that meet the 2026 California Energy Commission standards for insulation and solar reflectivity.  
- **Roof repair and patching** for storm damage (especially winter wind and summer heat spikes), leaks, broken shingles, and ponding water.  
- **Roof inspections and maintenance plans** that address flashing, sealants, vents, and gutter alignment—critical in LA’s hot, dry summers.  
- **Green roof and solar‑roof installations** that help commercial and residential owners qualify for rebates and tax incentives.  
- **Code compliance and permit assistance**, ensuring every roof meets Los Angeles Municipal Code (LAC) Section 6.0 and fire safety regulations.  

These services are typically bundled with a 1‑year workmanship warranty, and many contractors offer extended roofing or energy‑efficiency guarantees.

## How to Choose a Provider  
- **Verify licensing and insurance**: In California, a contractor must hold a valid C-3 (General Contractor) license and carry liability insurance plus workers’ comp.  
- **Check reviews and references**: Look for recent, local testimonials on Google, Yelp, and the BBB; ask for photo archives of past projects in the Hollywood Hills, Downtown, or Santa Monica areas.  
- **Demand a written, itemized estimate** that lists materials, labor, permits, and disposal fees.  
- **Assess response time**: A reputable roofer should reply within 24 hours and provide a site visit within 3 days.  
- **Confirm warranties**: Ensure the contract names both a workmanship warranty (typically 5–10 years) and a product warranty (material‑specific).  

## Typical Costs and What Affects Price  
Roofing costs in Los Angeles vary by material, roof size, and complexity. In 2026, typical ranges are:  

- **Asphalt shingles**: $4–$8 per square (100 sq ft) for basic to premium grades.  
- **Metal or composite panels**: $8–$15 per square, depending on thickness and finish.  
- **Slate or tile**: $12–$20 per square, plus additional labor for historic or steep‑pitch roofs.  

Factors that drive price include:  
- **Roof pitch and complexity** (steep slopes or intricate rooflines increase labor).  
- **Material choice and brand** (premium or custom‑color shingles cost more).  
- **Site accessibility** (roofs on high‑rise buildings or in dense neighborhoods may require lifts or scaffolds).  
- **Recycling fees** for old roofing materials, especially in environmentally‑aware L.A. districts.  

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring  
- What is the total projected cost, and does it include all permits and disposal fees?  
- How long will the project take, and what are the daily work hours?  
- Can you provide a written guarantee on both materials and labor, and what does it cover?  
- Will you handle the removal of the old roof, and will the debris be cleared from the property?  
- Do you have experience working on historic or landmark buildings in Los Angeles?  

A clear, detailed contract and timely communication are the best indicators of a dependable roofer.
