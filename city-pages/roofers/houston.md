# Find Trusted Roofing Contractors in Houston, TX

## What Houston Roofers Services Cover

In Houston, roofing professionals handle a broad spectrum of services tailored to the city’s humid climate and frequent storms.  Typical offerings include full‑siding replacement, roof repair, leak detection, and preventive sealing for both residential and commercial properties.  Many contractors also provide energy‑efficient options such as cool‑roof coatings, solar‑panel mounting, and green‑roof installations.  Because the region’s high humidity can accelerate rot and mold growth, Houston roofers often incorporate moisture‑control membranes and ventilation upgrades into their projects.

Beyond new construction, most Houston roofers specialize in storm‑damage mitigation.  After Hurricane Harvey and subsequent flooding, contractors now routinely perform roof‑to‑basement inspections, waterproofing, and structural reinforcement.  They also offer maintenance plans that include regular gutter cleaning, sealant touch‑ups, and thermographic scans to catch hidden issues early.

## How to Choose a Provider

- Verify the contractor is licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation and holds a valid Houston business registration.  
- Confirm liability insurance and workers’ compensation coverage; ask for the policy numbers.  
- Request a written estimate that itemizes materials, labor, permits, and cleanup.  
- Look for a clear response window—ideally 24–48 hours for inquiries and quotes.  
- Check online reviews on Google, Yelp, and the Better Business Bureau; aim for 4‑star or higher.  
- Ask about warranties: workmanship, materials, and energy‑efficiency guarantees.  

## Typical Costs and What Affects Price

The cost of a new roof in Houston varies by material, size, and complexity.  Asphalt shingles—the most common choice—generally range from **$5 to $10 per square foot** for a full roof replacement, while metal or slate roofs can climb to **$12–$25 per square foot**.  Labor, permitting, and disposal fees add to the total, often accounting for 30–40 % of the final bill.

Several factors push prices up or down:

- **Roof pitch and roof area:** Steeper roofs or larger spaces increase labor and material needs.  
- **Material choice:** Eco‑friendly or specialty coatings command a premium.  
- **Storm protection upgrades:** Adding impact‑resistant panels or additional flashing can raise costs.  
- **Site accessibility:** Roofs that are difficult to reach or have complex skylights require more time and equipment.  
- **Local permitting and inspections:** Houston’s building codes can vary by district, affecting permit fees.  

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring

- What is your experience with Houston’s specific weather conditions (hurricanes, heat, humidity)?  
- Can you provide references from recent local projects?  
- How do you handle unforeseen damage discovered during installation?  
- What is the projected timeline from start to finish?  
- Do you offer a written warranty, and what does it cover?

Take a few minutes to evaluate each potential roofer with these points in mind. A thorough vetting process saves time, money, and headaches—especially in a city where roofs can face intense stress from both seasons.
